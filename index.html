Before you book.

Need something more specific? Call or message us and we’ll help you plan your visit.

Can I discuss a look before booking makeup?

Yes. Share the occasion, outfit colours and a few reference photos during your consultation so the artist can understand the look you have in mind.

How do I enquire about a group mehendi booking?

Contact us with the event date, venue area and approximate guest count so we can discuss availability and arrangements.

Are course fees and batch dates listed here?

Course schedules and fees can vary. Contact the studio for current course details and upcoming batches.

Can I ask about lehenga styling before visiting?

Yes. Send your event type, date and preferred colours to arrange a styling conversation.
